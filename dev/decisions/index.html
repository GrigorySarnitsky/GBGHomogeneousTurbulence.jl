Decision log

The recorded design decisions of the package, append-only and dated. The rest of the rationale is on the Architecture page.

Append-only; each entry stands on its own. Dates in YYYY-MM.

  1. (2026-06) Integrating-factor split with exact diffusion. The stiff viscous/diffusive part is integrated exactly by $\mathcal{E}(\boldsymbol{\kappa})$; only the advection is explicit. Removes the diffusive stability limit and preserves scheme order under linear forcing.

  2. (2026-06) Rotational (Lamb-vector) form for the advection. Cheap spectral advection; the reversible generator of the geometric lens. (The energy conservation this entry originally credited to the form is really the sharp mask's: under a truncating band the truncation is exactly Galerkin, so the discrete advection conserves kinetic energy — HouLi, which tapers rather than truncates, is deliberately non-conservative. §4.2.)

  3. (2026-06) IFLowStorageRK3 (Williamson 2N / RKW3) is the default; per-scheme CFL. Third-order, imaginary-axis stable, two registers per field; IFRK2 kept as a cheaper, viscously-stabilized option.

  4. (2026-06) One passive scalar $\varphi$, coupled in-stage. Velocity and scalar advance through the same RK stages to keep the passive coupling time-accurate.

  5. (2026-06) Lagrangian tracers by Heun-in-time; interpolation scheme is the accuracy axis. The scheme uses the start- and end-of-step velocities the solver already produces.

  6. (2026-07) Statistics extracted to GBGStreamingStatistics. The conditional/transition-density machinery is domain-neutral and shared across turbulence, cloud microphysics, and molecular dynamics; it is consumed, not owned (§5).

  7. (2026-07) Organising frame is specification × realization, not three co-equal axes. Method is fibered over Model; the two research disciplines (method-independence, statistical commutation) map onto the two kinds of variation (§3). (Revised from an earlier "System × Model × Method" framing after an adversarial review.)

  8. (2026-07) Mori–Zwanzig is the central lens, not a foundation. The code does not depend on it; it organizes understanding and is demonstrated, not assumed. The other lenses (GENERIC, the commuting square, RG) are read through it, and RG/universality is a hypothesis to test (§8).

  9. (2026-07) Precision and footprint are studied axes. Both precisions supported equally, the working point chosen by fitness test; the sufficiency frontier is a deliverable (§6).

  10. (2026-07) Particles are instruments with first-class numerics. The role is a probe; the trajectory scheme and sampling-consistency are fitness-tested Method work (§4.6).

  11. (2026-07) The run + warm-up layer is a first-class, present concern. Ease of setup and running is the primary quality metric, so the ergonomic front door is built now, not deferred (§2, §4.7).

  12. (2026-07) Single ARCHITECTURE.md (consumer archetype) + docs/src/notation.md; no FORMALIZATION.md. IT is an operator consumer; its claims are dynamical and pinned by tests (§10).

  13. (2026-07) warmup! detects stationarity on the dissipation $\varepsilon$ and the velocity-derivative skewness, not the kinetic energy. $\varepsilon(t)$ (the small scales, which settle last) must stop drifting over a trailing window; $k(t)$ is avoided because a constant-energy forcing clamps it and a fixed-injection forcing makes it ring. The skewness $S(t)$ (derivative_skewness) must also have plateaued with $\lvert S\rvert$ above a floor — the "real turbulence yet" gate, whose default floor is a permissive one: raise it to $0.4$ when the run must be strict, against the $\lvert S\rvert \approx 0.5$ of a developed cascade ($S$ runs from $0$ toward $\approx -0.5$). When the scalar is forced, $\langle\varphi^2\rangle$ must flatten too — the scalar reaches stationarity after the velocity. A fixed turnover/time/step budget is the explicit alternative. (Follows the warm-up literature.)

  14. (2026-07) Operator-free forcing construction. ConstantPower(injection_rate = 0.1) (and the other banded forcings) return a DeferredForcing capturing the parameters, materialized into the concrete AbstractForcing once Simulation builds the domain — so a case names its forcing before the grid exists. The operator-taking constructor is unchanged. (Originally the keyword was power and the return type ForcingSpec; both since renamed — power → target, ForcingSpec → DeferredForcing — decision 25.)

  15. (2026-07) VarianceRescaling can hold the band or the total energy (over = :band | :total). :band fixes the forced band's energy and lets the total float; :total rescales the band to pin the whole-field energy — hence the r.m.s. velocity $u'$, setting the scale of $\mathrm{Re}_\lambda$ (whose value still fluctuates with $\varepsilon$) — the constant-total-energy forcing convention. A modeling choice with two defensible answers, so it is a selectable option rather than a fixed default.

  16. (2026-07) Modules named from the Mori–Zwanzig lens; Equations split into Dynamics + Observables; Driver → SimulationRun. Since MZ is the central lens (§8), the physics concerns take their names from it — the dynamics (the generator/RHS) and its observables (the measured functions of state) — rather than from production-DNS convention (rhs/stats) or generic software terms. Equations conflated the two and under-described; splitting it makes each concern its own module and its own name. Realization and infrastructure concerns keep job-names (TimeIntegration, Forcing, SimulationState, and the run layer SimulationRun, paired with SimulationState).

  17. (2026-07) Consume GBGPeriodicFields's fused domain; the velocity-derivative skewness becomes a coordinate-free strain invariant. Four fused operations were requested of, and shipped by, PF — combine! (axpby), weighted_inner, symmetric_gradient, det — and wired in. The combine! op replaces the hand-rolled RK/OU field_data broadcasts (charter invariant 2). The weighted_inner op fuses the per-step band energy in the forcings and expresses scalar_dissipation as the Dirichlet energy, with no $\nabla\varphi$ materialized. And derivative_skewness becomes the normalized third invariant of the strain rate $\boldsymbol{S}$, $3C_S\langle\det\boldsymbol{S}\rangle/\langle\boldsymbol{S}:\boldsymbol{S}\rangle^{3/2}$, with the exact isotropy constant $C_S = \tfrac{4}{7}\sqrt{15/2}\approx 1.5649$ reproducing the classical $S\approx-0.5$ — the axis-picking longitudinal form was coordinate-dependent, the invariant is the honest object, and symmetric_gradient forms the strain without the full velocity-gradient tensor. The physical strain tensor is unavoidable (cubic ⇒ non-Parseval), so this diagnostic allocates its rank-2 buffers per call (sampling cadence) rather than keeping them resident — footprint over compute.

  18. (2026-07) Phenomenology — a reference-law library, author-year naming, extraction-bound. A new submodule (§4.8) collecting the established turbulence phenomenology — model spectra, structure-function laws, dimensionless-constant fits — as one strategy family per quantity: each law family an abstract type, each parametrization an author-year preset (Spectra.Pope2000), disambiguated on three axes (quantity → submodule, paper → author-year, same-year collision → a trailing letter). It is the predicted counterpart to Observables — a provider the validation layer reads, not a comparer — and holds our own fits beside the literature on equal footing. Built inside this package but designed to be cut into a standalone GBGTurbulencePhenomenology with no rewrite (the GBGStreamingStatistics precedent): a dependency-light leaf — no GBGPeriodicFields, GBGStreamingStatistics, or dynamics dependency — that the umbrella does not re-export, with its own doc and API group. Extract when a second consumer appears.

  19. (2026-07) The measured-vs-predicted validation harness — the first Phenomenology consumer. The velocity energy spectrum is measured from a run (energy_spectrum, the halved GBGPeriodicFields radial spectrum) and the integral scale integral_scale from it; flow_parameters bridges a Simulation to a Phenomenology FlowParameters ($\nu$, $\varepsilon$, $u'$, $L_{11}$ from the measured state), and validate_spectrum(sim, model) compares the measured spectrum against a model spectrum evaluated at that state, returning a named tuple of the measured and predicted spectra and their relative $L^2$ discrepancy. The two sides are not the same kind of object and the comparison must convert: energy_spectrum returns the per-shell energy $E_n$, a model returns the density $E(\kappa)$, and $E_n \approx E(\kappa_n) \Delta\kappa$ with $\Delta\kappa = 2\pi/L$. The factor is invisible on the default $L = 2\pi$ box, where it is $1$, and silently wrong on any other. (Superseded 2026-08-23, owner-directed: energy_spectrum now returns the density — the shell energy over $\Delta\kappa$ — so the conversion seam this entry documents is gone; measured and modeled spectra are the same kind of object and compare directly. The bin-sum convention had survived precisely because the factor is 1 on the default box.) This realizes the comparison-machine path of §3 for spectra — the measured half is a GBGPeriodicFields reduction, so no GBGStreamingStatistics bridge is needed for it. The structure-function and scaling-constant paths (via radial_correlation and the scalar diagnostics) share the shape and are deferred.

  20. (2026-07) Resolution-based viscosity — the run layer consumes a Phenomenology law to build a run. Simulation now sets $\nu$ from the grid by default (no viscosity): it reads the dissipation the box sustains from the box-normalized dissipation law Scalings.Li2008 ($k/(L\varepsilon)^{2/3} \approx 0.9$, a box-normalized group — distinct from the classical dissipation anomaly $C_\varepsilon = \varepsilon L_{11}/u'^3$ on the integral scale, related only on a common length by $G = \tfrac{3}{2} C_\varepsilon^{-2/3}$ — calibrated to JHTDB), then sets $\nu$ so the resolution criterion $\kappa_{\max}\eta$ equals the κ_max_η option (default 2) — the highest Reynolds number consistent with that resolution criterion. This is the construction half of the consume-and-fit loop (§4.7): laws are read to build runs, not only to validate them. The run is forced-and-stationary by default (a VarianceRescaling holds the total energy at the value given by the kinetic_energy keyword, default 1.5, i.e. $u'=1$); an explicit viscosity opts back into direct control. (Superseded in the holder, not in the substance: decision 58 made VarianceController the default. The calibration constraint — the run must really hold $k$ at the assumed value — is unchanged, and the controller holds it to a tolerance rather than exactly.) It does not breach the extraction boundary — Li2008 stays a pure scalar law with no GBGPeriodicFields / GBGStreamingStatistics / dynamics dependency; only the run layer (never Phenomenology) knows both sides. The accuracy target is $\kappa_{\max}\eta$ (spectral content at the highest evolved wavenumber), not $\eta/\Delta x$ (geometric): the former is dealiasing-aware through $\kappa_{\max} = f \tfrac{N}{2}\tfrac{2\pi}{L}$ (max_wavenumber), which is correct because accuracy itself depends on the dealiasing.

  21. (2026-07) The physics-first carving is checked against a formal structural model (docs/architecture.als). The module carving and the Mori–Zwanzig role wiring were re-examined against an explicit relational-logic model (built and checked in Alloy) under an adversarial multi-lens review — a design-time artifact, deliberately not a shipped ledger — to counter a pull toward software-shaped, object-first structure. The model carries only structure: the dependency graph (checked against the actual using edges), the state as one product of typed slots on a single module, the Model ladder as a coarsening poset with an explicit fine→coarse projection, the reversible/irreversible/source split of the generator wired to the observables that measure it, closure required exactly where a rung is a proper projection, and the passive tracer kept distinct from an inertial (slipping) phase. It carries no dynamics — the nonlinear operator, the exact rates, the memory integral, the symplectic bracket stay with the numerical oracle (§10, decision 12) — so it is not a FORMALIZATION.md by another route. Four adversarial passes converged: the structural claims are mutually consistent and every self-check is non-vacuous (perturb its premise and the check fails). The exercise confirmed the carving is internally consistent and surfaced two things now recorded: the four MZ roles are level-agnostic — abstractions the coarser Model rungs will populate, ready but not populated while DNS is the only built rung (not a speculative extension point, invariant 9) — and the state-realization discipline now folded into invariant 4.

  22. (2026-07) Mori–Zwanzig raised to foundation; the physics-first renames; this document rewritten. Following the structural verification (decision 21), Mori–Zwanzig is made the foundation of the architecture, not merely the central lens — reversing decision 8. The reversal is safe because the decomposition is exact (a projection is an identity, not an approximation); what stays investigated, never assumed is the closure that models the memory it leaves, so the foundation and the "investigate, don't assume" charter (invariant 6) are one commitment. It founds the architecture — the carving and the names — not the numerics, which are Navier–Stokes solved pseudospectrally regardless. The four roles take their role names: Dynamics → Generator, TimeIntegration → Propagator, and SimulationState split into SimulationDomain (the frame) and State (the field), completing and partly revising decision 16. Decision 16's "realization keeps job-names" is re-cut on the sharper line it was reaching for — a level-agnostic Mori–Zwanzig role wears its role name, a concrete mechanism wears its job — under which the propagator is a role present at every rung, not mere realization, so Propagator is correct while its schemes (IFLowStorageRK3) keep their descriptive names. ARCHITECTURE.md was rewritten whole (except this append-only log) to read holistically from the foundation down.

  23. (2026-07) Review-round refinements: Physics → FlowConditions folded into Generator, the forcing-protocol renames, and an open-systems sub-lens. A close read of the rewrite settled three changes. (a) The parameter bundle Physics — the viscosity, the Schmidt number, and the two forcings, the physical conditions that tune the generator, misfiled under the Propagator stepper — is renamed FlowConditions and folded into Generator, the right-hand side it parametrizes; this adds the honest Generator → Forcing dependency (the RHS includes the forcing source). A six-perspective naming panel chose FlowConditions over System (the bundle is a proper subset of the §3 System axis — it lacks the initial condition and the domain — so a type named for the axis while holding less is ambiguous and imprecise) and over GeneratorParameters (relational, and imprecise once a coarser rung's generator gains closure coefficients). (b) The AbstractForcing protocol methods are made grammatically uniform, verb-first: set_forcing_rate!, add_forcing_source!, prepare_forcing!, project_forcing!. (c) The open-systems / non-equilibrium reading is recorded as a sub-lens under Mori–Zwanzig (§8), not new state types: a six-tradition panel (unanimous) confirmed the system/environment split is the Mori–Zwanzig resolved/modeled cut, but that whatever a model retains of the environment is a finite Markovian surrogate on the system's own side — a modeled slot on the one State, never a separate 'environment state' — so a stateful forcing's internal state homes there when StochasticOU is de-archived, and no type is minted now (invariant 9).

  24. (2026-07) The domain module is SimulationDomain, its concrete type FixedDomain. Decision 22 split the frame into a SimulationDomain module; naming its concrete type took one more cut, because Julia will not let a module cleanly re-export a type of its own name — using ..SimulationDomain: SimulationDomain binds the module and shadows the type, so a bare SimulationDomain(...) constructor call stops resolving (verified empirically). The module keeps the user-facing concern name SimulationDomain (the discretized box a run lives on); its type takes a name for what it is among the alternatives. Weighed against SimpleDomain (relational and vague — simple next to what?) and PeriodicCube (a property the deferred sheared frame also has), the choice is FixedDomain: the fixed, unsheared, orthogonal cube with time-independent, separable wavenumbers. It names the very axis the deferred ShearedDomain (the Rogallo moving frame, §7) varies — fixed versus sheared, separable versus time-dependent — so the two type names read as one family. FlowConditions needs no such care: a type, not a module, it cannot collide.

  25. (2026-07) Design-review batch: the recovery split, ForcingSpec → DeferredForcing, a scalar spectrum IC, and precision-aware Hou–Li damping. Four refinements settled on a reading of the API. (a) The fused velocity_vorticity! is split into the sibling recoveries velocity_to_physical! and vorticity_to_physical!: its two halves share no intermediate — curl-then-transform for the vorticity, copy-then-transform for the velocity, over separate buffers — so the split carries no compute or memory penalty and reads as one convention with scalar_to_physical! (X_to_physical! produces the physical X, its source shown in the signature). (b) ForcingSpec is renamed DeferredForcing, naming the object by its meaning — a forcing specified before the domain exists and materialized once it does — rather than the spec abbreviation (superseding the name minted in decision 14). (c) random_scalar_spectral! gains a spectrum keyword mirroring random_solenoidal_velocity_spectral!, seeding the scalar from a target radial scalar-variance spectrum (an Obukhov–Corrsin form from Phenomenology) for spectrum-matched initial conditions and faster warm-up. (d) HouLi's strength defaults to nothing, meaning auto: resolved at kernel-build time to $-\log$ of the element type's machine epsilon ($\approx 36$ for Float64, $\approx 16$ for Float32), so the Nyquist mode always damps to that precision's epsilon — precision as a studied axis (invariant 4); an explicit strength overrides.

  26. (2026-07) The state reified as ResolvedFields; the per-step scratch relocated to Propagator. The DNS state — the evolving velocity $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ and optional scalar $\hat\varphi$ — is made first-class as ResolvedFields (module State), completing the Mori–Zwanzig triad whose State leg had been missing: the fields were scattered across the Simulation value while the diagnostics read the physical scratch as if it were state (kinetic_energy(sim) read buffers.velocity). It is a bare, inert record with every operation external — the panel's ruling, which adopts the category lens's guardrail (arrows primary, no methods on the carrier) and rejects its dissolution. ResolvedFields holds only the spectral truth ($\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$, $\hat\varphi$); the physical velocity, vorticity, and scalar are a Method-side derived view, never stored, so the state cannot go stale — and because the physical chart is rung-specific (EDQNM has no phases to invert), keeping it out of the state keeps the record fit to lift, unchanged, to one slot of the future product-of-slots State (§7, abstraction-ready, not built). The observables become thin functions of the natural representation with no State dependency: kinetic_energy/scalar_variance/derivative_skewness/energy_spectrum are Parseval reductions of $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$/$\hat\varphi$, dissipation curls into scratch and reduces, and the run layer prepares what each needs — the mirror of the generator, both now thin externals over bare fields. Consequently the monolithic FieldBuffers is dissolved: the generator's operators take their individual scratch fields (naming no bundle), so the scratch is free to live with the scheme that owns it as IntegratorBuffers (module Propagator), built by allocate_buffers dispatching on the concrete scheme; ScalarBuffers stays a nested optional sub-bundle. The name ResolvedFields (over ResolvedState / DNSState / FieldState) follows the FixedDomain axis-lesson taken to the slot level: the module State carries the level-agnostic role, so the concrete slot is named for its representation — the resolved fields — with FilteredFields (LES/FDF) and a spectrum record (EDQNM) its eventual siblings in the product. A six-lens naming panel with an adversarial pass fixed the slate (IntegratorBuffers, and the scratch-field names product_physical/product_spectral over the velocity-only lamb). (Amended by decisions 30–31: the physical chart the diagnostics read is now typed — the ObservablesCache in State — the recoveries live with the state, the assembly scratch on the ResolvedGenerator, and IntegratorBuffers holds only the scheme's registers; the individual-scratch-arguments discipline this entry set applies unchanged to the internals.)

  27. (2026-07) Phase-shift dealiasing, on GBGPeriodicFields' translation_kernel. Adds PhaseShift as an opt-in DealiasingStrategy (the default stays the rotational form + spherical $2/3$). Each quadratic nonlinear term is assembled twice — on the grid and on a grid displaced half a cell — and averaged, cancelling the aliasing images that wrap in an odd number of axes; a spherical truncation at $\kappa_{\max} = \tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{3}N\cdot\tfrac{2\pi}{L} \approx 0.94 \kappa_{\mathrm{Ny}}$ removes the surviving two-axis images (a single half-cell shift is exact only in 1-D; Patterson & Orszag 1971, Rogallo 1981). It keeps a $\sqrt{2}$ wider band than the $2/3$ rule ($\approx 2.8\times$ the modes in the retained sphere) at $2\times$ the nonlinear-term transforms and a displaced-evaluation scratch field. This realizes the deferred D6 assembly generalization (§7) for this sibling: DealiasingStrategy now dispatches the whole $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}\to$ dealiased-term computation, not only a post-multiply kernel. The displacement is built once as two translation_kernels (the shift theorem's phase, a GBGPeriodicFields translation primitive whose Nyquist even/odd split makes the kernel a real field); because the cutoff is sub-Nyquist the Nyquist-attenuation caveat never bites. Verified against a zero-padded oracle — exact to machine precision across the retained band on a field whose raw product is 44 % aliased.

  28. (2026-07) Defaults live only at the Simulation front door; observables read the state; buffers are per-scheme. A round of API tightening after a fresh docs read.

    • Defaults only in SimulationRun. The low-level types carry no convenience defaults — FixedDomain, FlowConditions, the initial-condition seeders, and Tracers require their arguments; the Simulation front door supplies the run-layer values. Model/preset types (Pope2000, HouLi, …) keep their canonical parameters; rng may default anywhere. (This removed a laundered smell — an IC seeder that defaulted kinetic_energy = 0.5 while the front door supplied 1.5, once documented "by design" rather than fixed.)
    • scalar_type → element_type (the keyword names the array element type, not the passive scalar's type); Tracers(domain; positions), so backend and precision flow from the domain (all-keyword past domain since decision 52).
    • step! advances the State (ResolvedFields), not loose $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ / $\hat\varphi$.
    • Observables are functions of the state, not the Simulation. No f(sim) form: an observable taking a Simulation reached across state / buffers / conditions, smearing the single source of truth (decision 26); the run layer supplies the explicit state variables.
    • allocate_buffers is per-scheme and exact (invariant 4): each scheme allocates only the registers it uses; over-allocation is a hard error. The friction of the now-explicit FlowConditions is the standing argument for a reified Generator operator (§7). (Realized: decision 31.)

  29. (2026-07) The RK3 stage ordering: both right-hand sides before either update; the dealiasing × scheme convergence matrix. The IFLowStorageRK3 stage loop updated and rotated $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ before assembling the scalar's advection. A mask strategy never reads $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ there, but PhaseShift's displaced evaluation does (decision 27) — so the displaced flux paired the next stage's velocity with this stage's scalar, breaking the odd-axis alias cancellation at $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$ and degrading the scalar from third to first order (measured self-convergence ratios 2.02 against 7.97 for the velocity; IFRK2's call sites were consistent, and the term-level padding oracle cannot see a call-site mismatch — it checks the assembly with matched inputs). The comment that had licensed the old order ("u and φ̂ are untouched above") was written before PhaseShift made the assembly read $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ — an invalidated premise, not a wrong derivation. Both right-hand sides are now evaluated at the stage state before either field is updated (bit-identical for the mask strategies), and the suite carries the dealiasing × scheme convergence matrix: both fields must converge at scheme order under every strategy. The bug is the motivating instance for decision 31's seam — a stage that reads a mid-update intermediate is a malformed factorization, which a reified right-hand side makes unrepresentable.

  30. (2026-07) The observation layer: ObservablesCache — epoch-dated, memoized charts over the state; the recoveries move to State. The physical recoveries are the chart map of the state — they read only $(\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}, \hat\varphi)$ and the frame — so they home in State (Generator imports them for its displaced evaluations; the new Generator → State edge respects the include order). ObservablesCache memoizes their values: preallocated slots (:velocity, :vorticity), each carrying the fields epoch it was computed at; an accessor (physical_velocity, physical_vorticity) returns the stored field when current and recomputes it first otherwise — the deposit-or-recompute contract — and step! advances the epoch as it completes. An out-of-band mutation is declared through fields_modified! — named for what was changed, the resolved fields, which is the plain reading and the one the code follows. (Two corrections to this entry's original text, both recorded rather than made silently. The verb was named resolved_fields_modified! when the decision was taken; decision 24 shortened it, and the sweep updated the log to the current name as decisions 48 and 53 did for their vocabulary. And the entry credited the name to SciML's u_modified! → derivative_discontinuity! lesson, "name the verb for the violated invariant, not the mutated slot" — a rationale that is backwards for this verb: fields_modified! names the mutated slot, and it earns its place by saying plainly what a caller did, not by describing a downstream consequence the caller would have to reason about.) the sanctioned mutators call it themselves, and the seeders' docstrings direct hand-run loops to it. This kills the step! silent-precondition footgun — there is no caller prefill left to forget. Epoch granularity is deliberately coarse: one counter per dynamical factor of the tower (fields, particles), no finer — every production memoization precedent surveyed (LAMMPS computes' timestep tags, OpenFOAM's registry event numbers, Fluidsim's iteration counters) uses a single counter, within-step consistency is the seam's job (decision 31), and an over-fine epoch system is itself a silent-staleness surface. Observables are opt-in (an undeclared one errors — resident GPU memory is a choice), and the cache's shared spectral scratch replaces two IntegratorBuffers slots — net one resident spectral vector fewer than before the layer. Naming: ObservablesCache was fixed by a six-lens panel (5–1 over DerivedQuantities, upheld by an adversarial pass) with a license clause the docstring carries — each slot holds a derived field's values, on the grid lattice or at the tracer positions; particle content never enters — a recorded rename trigger (the first particle-sourced slot, e.g. a number density, reopens the name), and a recorded guard: $\varepsilon$, $k$, $E(\kappa)$ never become resident slots (statistics belong to GBGStreamingStatistics; occasional diagnostics allocate per call). Kills, not to be re-proposed: Observations (collides with Observables), DerivedViews/StateViews (a Julia view cannot be stale), Charts (these are non-invertible functionals, not manifold observables), Diagnostics (the community word for scalar time series), Snapshot (asserts restart-copy structure), DerivedQuantities (indiscriminate extension).

  31. (2026-07) The generator reified: ResolvedGenerator and the six-verb protocol; schemes shrink to stage arithmetic. Closes §7's deferred item on its own terms — the reification now carries what the loose functions could not: the assembly scratch (the quadratic products, the PhaseShift buffers, the forcing-rate arrays), the conditions, and the step lifecycle. The protocol: prepare! (open the step — advance forcing state, arm linear rates), velocity_rhs! / scalar_rhs! (each field's explicit right-hand side at a stage state: the dealiased nonlinear term plus the additive forcing source), velocity_integrating_factor! / scalar_integrating_factor! (each field's exact linear subflow — the generator is the semilinear split $\partial_t \hat q = A\hat q + N(\hat q)$ with a diagonal, exact linear operator, the exponential-scheme family's special case), and finalize! (close the step — the post-step forcing projections). The rhs/factor pairs are the required core; prepare!/finalize! default to no-ops (a two-tier required/optional protocol). finalize! on the generator is a recorded deviation from the solver-library precedent (the SciML/PETSc/SUNDIALS right-hand-side objects are pure evaluators, with post-step action in callback layers), adopted because the projection is part of the step's contract, not of observation: the observables, the diagnostics, and the tracer advance must see the forced end-of-step state, and molecular-dynamics practice places exactly this operation inside the step (a constraint's velocity-projection stage, a thermostat's end-of-step rescale, the trailing factor of a symmetric splitting). The signature becomes step!(scheme, state, generator, domain, buffers, derived, dt): both schemes now contain no physics — no viscosity, no forcing calls, no conditions — and Propagator drops its Forcing dependency. The old public assembly trio (velocity_nonlinear!, scalar_nonlinear!, integrating_factor!) demotes to internals behind the protocol, retiring the eight-argument assembly signatures. The name extends the axis convention — ResolvedFields ↔ ResolvedGenerator, resolved against filtered, with FilteredGenerator (carrying its closure) the eventual LES sibling — and the run layer's _resolve_forcing was renamed _materialize_forcing, reserving resolved for that axis against DeferredForcing's materialization vocabulary. Every migration stage was verified bit-identical on fixed-seed trajectories (all three dealiasing strategies and the StochasticOU-forced case) against the pre-migration code.

  32. (2026-07) Forcing hooks own their scratch; the lifecycle order is contract. The four-hook interface no longer receives the scheme's buffer bundle ("for interface uniformity" — the smell decision 26's discipline predicted): set_forcing_rate!, project_forcing!, prepare_forcing!, and add_forcing_source! take only what they act on, and StochasticOU owns its noise scratch (one field in physical and one in spectral representation, allocated on first use like its $\hat{\boldsymbol{f}}$ state) instead of reaching into buffers.product_* by field name — at the recorded price of two resident fields for a velocity-forcing instance, accepted to sever the one cross-component reach-in (the extended-system discipline: a thermostat never touches the scheme's registers; the OU re-add stays gated on a GPU-tuned generator either way). The hook lifecycle — prepare and rate before the stages, source at every stage, projection after the completed step — is documented as part of the AbstractForcing contract, with the rate and the source recorded as two channels of one forcing term (the exactly linear part in the factor, the remainder at stages). This stage deliberately ran before the generator reification, against the migration plan's original order: the borrow had to be severed before the borrowed scratch could move homes.   35. (2026-07) A recorded cost is not a minimal one: every audited cell is held against a physical floor, and the residual distance must be claimed.   1. (2026-08, user-requested) A second trajectory scheme: variable-step Adams–Bashforth. The Lagrangian right-hand side is a deposited observable — every iteration produces it whether or not a scheme wants it — so a multistep method buys order out of storage the run already fills, at no extra field evaluation and no extra interpolation, where EndpointHeun pays one interpolation per step.     Two departures, both forced here. Steps are not uniform — the advective-CFL loop changes $\Delta t$ every iteration, so the weights are recomputed per step as the integral of the Lagrange polynomial through the stored sample times, verified to reduce to the classical constants at uniform step. The startup bounds the order — TurTLE ramps AB1→AB4, but AB1 is Euler and measurement gave $\approx 2.0$ for the ramp against $\approx 3.0$ for an EndpointHeun bootstrap, so the bootstrap shipped. Fourth order over a fixed window would need a start of matching order, unavailable because it wants the velocity between step endpoints.

    The interpolation caveat was corrected the same day, having first read "raising the time order past the interpolation's smoothness buys nothing." That misattributes the mechanism. The interpolant perturbs the field by a $\Delta t$-independent $\epsilon$, so Grönwall gives a trajectory-error floor no timestep can cross — and the floor is set by the interpolant's reproduction degree, not its smoothness. Measured on a frozen ABC flow at $64^3$, $T = 1$: CatmullRom ($C^1$, degree 2) floors at $1.2\times10^{-5}$; LalescuSpline(3, 1) (same $C^1$, degree 3) at $2.6\times10^{-7}$; LagrangeSpline(3) ($C^0$, degree 7) at $1.2\times10^{-11}$. At fixed degree 7, $C^0$ and $C^3$ gave identical time convergence, so smoothness caps nothing here — it earns its keep in readouts that differentiate the interpolant.

(2026-08, user-directed) A state-dependent forcing rate belongs in the source, not in the integrating factor: VarianceController is the new default, and VarianceRescaling is kept as a documented first-order reference. The audit that followed the bug hunt found the default Simulation integrating at first order on a third-order scheme, and the cause was routing rather than any error in the schemes or the forcing protocol. Both are worth separating, because only one of them was a defect.

    The genuine defect: ConstantPower published a rate that it recomputed every step from the current state. A rate folded into $\mathcal{E}$ is integrated exactly — but exactness is a statement about a steady $A$. Freezing a state-dependent one at the step's start makes the step a left-endpoint rectangle rule on $\int A\,\mathrm dt$: local error $O(\Delta t^2)$, global order 1, and the collapse is silent because nothing about the code looks wrong. Moving it to add_forcing_source!, where the scheme's own stages evaluate it, restored 3.01 from 1.02. The protocol needed no change — the four hooks were already the right four. The fix was to stop claiming exactness the strategy could not deliver, which is why uses_linear_rate is now uses_steady_linear_rate: the old name asserted linearity in the field, and the load-bearing property is steadiness in time.

    Not a defect, and kept: VarianceRescaling's first order. A post-step rescale is Lie–Trotter operator splitting, so it is first-order by construction and no routing can save it. It is a published method (Sullivan, Mahalingam & Kerr 1994) a reader may need to reproduce, and holding a band's value exactly is occasionally what is wanted. It stays, with the caveat stated in its own docstring rather than discovered by a convergence study, and a test pins its order below 1.5 so the caveat cannot rot into a claim.

    The replacement is VarianceController, the constant-$k$ controller of Bassenne, Urzay, Park & Moin (2016, Eq. 20): $A = [\varepsilon - G(k - k_\infty)/t_\ell]/(2k_f)$ — the whole field's variance $k$ in the restoring term, the forced band's $k_f$ normalizing, and the two are not interchangeable (see the postscript). Its marginal term $\varepsilon/2k_f$ reinjects exactly what dissipation removes, and its proportional term gives $\mathrm dk/\mathrm dt = -G(k - k_\infty)/t_\ell$ — exponential relaxation to a stable attractor. That is the structural difference from the rescale, not a numerical refinement: the clamp imposes the target and destroys the fluctuation of the quantity it holds, while the controller restores toward it and leaves the fluctuation to the flow. Molecular dynamics settled the same question the same way — naive velocity rescaling versus a thermostat that extends the dynamics with feedback — and the analogy is recorded in the docstrings because a reader arriving from that literature will recognize the pair immediately.

    The naming is deliberate: Variance, not Energy, because one implementation serves both fields and the held quantity is each field's $L^2$ observable — the kinetic energy for the velocity, the scalar variance for the scalar. Naming it for the velocity's case would have made the scalar's the exception. Measured after the change: unforced, Linear, ConstantPower, VarianceController and the default Simulation all converge at 3.01; VarianceRescaling at 1.02. The controller was verified to converge on its target from both sides ($k_0 = 0.4$ and $k_0 = 2.6$ toward $1.5$), which a clamp cannot be said to do at all.

    A postscript, because the round nearly shipped the wrong physics. The first implementation put the band variance in both places of that formula — normalizing by $k_f$ (right) and restoring toward $k_f$ (wrong). Both readings are dimensionally sound and the code looked symmetric, which is what made it survive review: it is a category error, not an arithmetic one, between the quantity a forcing acts on and the quantity it holds. The consequence was precise and invisible from the test suite — the run held the band at $1.5$ and let $k$ settle about 30% above it, silently breaking the grid-mode $\nu$ calibration that decision 15 had made conditional on the run really holding $k$. The /audit-changes run over this very commit caught it, by executing the default configuration and measuring $k$ rather than reading the diff. Two things now hold it: the whole-field and band reductions are separated and commented at the point of use, and a witness compares the applied amplitude against both candidate formulas, asserting it matches one and not the other — a steady state cannot satisfy it by luck.
