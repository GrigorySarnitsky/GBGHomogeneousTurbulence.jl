Generator

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator — Module
Generator

The flow's equations of motion — the incompressible Navier–Stokes right-hand side in rotational form (the de-aliased, Leray-projected nonlinear term), the passive-scalar advection, and the exact viscous integrating factor.

Its contents (each formula lives in the operation's own docstring):

  • FlowConditions — the physical conditions the right-hand side is built from (the viscosity, the Schmidt number, the forcings).
  • velocity_to_physical!, vorticity_to_physical!, scalar_to_physical! — recover the velocity, vorticity, and scalar in physical representation from their spectral representation.
  • velocity_nonlinear!, scalar_nonlinear! — assemble the de-aliased, Leray-projected momentum and scalar nonlinear terms.
  • integrating_factor! — build the exact viscous/diffusive multiplier for the linear diffusion term.

Each field-transforming or nonlinear-assembly operation takes the domain (a FixedDomain) and the individual preallocated scratch field(s) it writes into as its final argument(s); integrating_factor! needs no scratch field — it takes domain with the scalar diffusivity, the step dt, and an optional forcing rate.

Flow conditions

The physical conditions the generator is built from — viscosity, Schmidt number, and the velocity and scalar forcings — that step! consumes.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.FlowConditions — Type
FlowConditions(; viscosity, schmidt = 1.0, velocity_forcing = NoForcing(),
               scalar_forcing = NoForcing())

The physical conditions the generator is built from — the kinematic viscosity $\nu$ (viscosity; the Reynolds number is $\mathrm{Re} = 1/\nu$), the Schmidt number $\mathrm{Sc}$ (schmidt; the scalar diffusivity is $\varkappa = \nu/\mathrm{Sc}$), and the velocity and scalar forcing strategies (velocity_forcing, scalar_forcing, both AbstractForcing). Consumed by the time step (step!).

Nonlinear-term assembly

The de-aliased, Leray-projected rotational-form momentum term, and the conservative scalar-advection term.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.velocity_nonlinear! — Function
velocity_nonlinear!(nonlinear, u, ω, domain, product_physical, product_spectral)

Assemble the de-aliased, Leray-projected nonlinear term of the momentum equation into nonlinear, from the velocity u and vorticity ω in physical representation.

In physical space the rotational form writes the convective term as the Lamb vector, through the identity

\[(\boldsymbol{u}\cdot\nabla)\boldsymbol{u} = \nabla\big(\tfrac12\lvert\boldsymbol{u}\rvert^{2}\big) - \boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega},\]

so that, with the Leray–Helmholtz projection $\mathbb{P}$ onto divergence-free fields absorbing the pressure and the $\nabla(\tfrac12\lvert\boldsymbol{u}\rvert^{2})$ gradient, the momentum equation becomes

\[\partial_t\boldsymbol{u} = \mathbb{P}\,(\boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega}) + \nu\nabla^{2}\boldsymbol{u} + \boldsymbol{f}.\]

The term computed here is the spectral image of $\mathbb{P}\,(\boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega})$, with the de-alias kernel domain.dealias (the sharp $2/3$ rule by default) controlling the aliasing of the quadratic product. The Lamb vector is held in the scratch product_physical (a vector field in physical representation) and its transform in product_spectral (a vector field in spectral representation), so the operation allocates nothing.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.scalar_nonlinear! — Function
scalar_nonlinear!(nonlinear, u, φ, domain, product_physical, product_spectral)

Assemble the de-aliased advection term of the passive-scalar equation into nonlinear, from the velocity u and scalar φ in physical representation.

In conservative form the advection is $-\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol{u}\varphi)$ (equal to $-\boldsymbol{u}\cdot\nabla\varphi$ since $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{u} = 0$); the conservative form is used because it conserves the scalar mean exactly — the divergence has no $\boldsymbol{\kappa}=\boldsymbol 0$ component, and the de-alias kernel domain.dealias (the sharp $2/3$ rule by default) leaves that mode untouched. It is computed pseudospectrally as $-\,\mathrm{dealias}\big(\mathrm{i}\boldsymbol{\kappa}\cdot\widehat{\boldsymbol{u}\varphi}\big)$, the kernel controlling the aliasing of the quadratic product. The scalar flux $\boldsymbol{u}\varphi$ reuses the momentum scratch — product_physical in physical representation, product_spectral in spectral.

Integrating factor

The exact per-mode diffusive propagator — ν for the velocity, ν/Sc for the scalar — optionally folding in a linear forcing rate.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.integrating_factor! — Function
integrating_factor!(factor, domain, diffusivity, dt, rate = nothing)

Write the exact diffusive integrating factor into factor (a scalar field in spectral representation). Over a step $\Delta t$ (dt) the diffusion equation $\partial_t f = D\nabla^{2} f$ is solved exactly in spectral representation by the per-mode multiplier

\[\mathcal{E}(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) = \exp\!\big(-D\,\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2}\,\Delta t\big),\]

so multiplying a field by factor advances its viscous/diffusive part exactly (no stability restriction). The domain (a FixedDomain) supplies the squared-wavenumber field $\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2}$ (domain.squared_wavenumber); in spectral representation $-\nabla^{2}$ acts as multiplication by it. The diffusivity $D$ (diffusivity) is the kinematic viscosity $\nu$ for the velocity and $\nu/\mathrm{Sc}$ for the scalar ($\mathrm{Sc}$ the Schmidt number).

When a linear forcing rate $A(\boldsymbol{\kappa})$ is supplied (rate, a real array; from the set_forcing_rate! of a Linear/ConstantPower forcing), it is folded into the linear operator, $\mathcal{E}(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) = \exp\!\big((A(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) - D\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2})\,\Delta t\big)$ — exact for the linear forcing, so the integrator keeps its order.
