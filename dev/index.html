GBGIsotropicTurbulence.jl

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.jl is a small, focused pseudospectral direct-numerical-simulation (DNS) package for homogeneous isotropic turbulence on a three-dimensional periodic domain: an incompressible velocity field $\boldsymbol{u}$ together with a single passive scalar $\varphi$, each optionally forced, advanced by a choice of integrating-factor time-stepping scheme, with Lagrangian particle tracking and Eulerian + Lagrangian statistics.

It is built on GBGPeriodicFields, which supplies every field operation — transforms, spectral differential operators, convolution, interpolation, and reductions. GBGPeriodicFields is deliberately time- and particle-free; this package is the consumer that owns time integration, forcing, the scalar transport, particle tracking, and the statistics. The GPU is the primary target, so the time loop is in-place and allocation-free over a preallocated workspace.

Installation

This is a private, unregistered package. With repository access:

using Pkg

Pkg.add(url="https://github.com/GrigorySarnitsky/GBGIsotropicTurbulence.jl")

Then:

using GBGIsotropicTurbulence

What it solves

With $\boldsymbol{u}$ the velocity, $\boldsymbol{\omega} = \nabla\times\boldsymbol{u}$ the vorticity, $\nu$ the kinematic viscosity, $\mathbb{P}$ the Leray–Helmholtz projection onto divergence-free fields, and $\boldsymbol{f}$ a forcing, the momentum equation is advanced in rotational form,

\[\partial_t \boldsymbol{u} = \mathbb{P}\,(\boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega}) + \nu\nabla^{2}\boldsymbol{u} + \boldsymbol{f}, \qquad \nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{u} = 0,\]

and the passive scalar $\varphi$ by the advection–diffusion equation

\[\partial_t \varphi = -\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol{u}\,\varphi) + \frac{\nu}{\mathrm{Sc}}\nabla^{2}\varphi + f_\varphi,\]

with $\mathrm{Sc}$ the Schmidt number. In both equations the diffusive (stiff) term is integrated exactly through an integrating factor, the nonlinear term explicitly by a Runge–Kutta stage combination, and the quadratic product is de-aliased by the $2/3$ rule.

Work in progress

The package is being built in vertical slices. The coupled velocity + passive-scalar solver is in place, advanced by the integrating-factor schemes (IFLowStorageRK3 — the default — and IFRK2), with the full forcing menu NoForcing, BandRescaling, Linear, ConstantPower, and the stochastic StochasticOU (Eswaran–Pope), and Lagrangian particle tracking (Tracers). The Eulerian and Lagrangian statistics are being added.

Components

  • SimulationState: the reusable spectral machinery (SpectralOperators), the preallocated buffers (Workspace), and the random divergence-free initial condition.
  • Dynamics: the flow's equations of motion — the rotational-form Navier–Stokes right-hand side, the passive-scalar advection, and the exact viscous integrating factor.
  • Observables: the measured functions of state (kinetic_energy, dissipation, derivative_skewness, scalar_variance, scalar_dissipation).
  • Forcing: the pluggable energy-injection strategies under AbstractForcing.
  • TimeIntegration: the pluggable integrating-factor schemes under AbstractIntegrator.
  • Particles: Lagrangian tracer tracking — second-order advection and field sampling along trajectories, with a pluggable interpolation scheme.
