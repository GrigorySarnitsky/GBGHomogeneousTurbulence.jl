# API overview

The package is an umbrella module (`GBGIsotropicTurbulence`) assembled from submodules. Its **re-exported**
submodules, in dependency order, are `SimulationDomain` → `State` → `Observables` → `Cache` → `Forcing` →
`Generator` → `Propagator` → `Particles` → `SimulationRun`; the extraction-bound `Phenomenology` leaf
leads the include order but is **not** re-exported (reach it opt-in). The umbrella re-exports the public
surface of the listed submodules, so `using GBGIsotropicTurbulence` brings everything into scope.

- [GBGIsotropicTurbulence](api/GBGIsotropicTurbulence.md): the top-level module and public re-export surface.
- [SimulationDomain](api/SimulationDomain.md): the discretized periodic box a run lives on — the `FixedDomain` (with its choice of `DealiasingStrategy`), the scratch-field allocators, and the resolved-wavenumber ceiling `max_wavenumber`.
- [State](api/State.md): the evolving state (`ResolvedFields` — the velocity and scalar in spectral representation, the single source of truth) and the random initial conditions that seed it — a divergence-free velocity and a mean-zero scalar.
- [Observables](api/Observables.md): the measured functions of state — the energy and dissipation diagnostics of the flow and the scalar (the kinetic energy and its dissipation rate among them), the developed-turbulence markers, the spectrum-derived scales, the maximum velocity component that sets the CFL step, and the field-valued vorticity recovery.
- [Cache](api/Cache.md): the `ObservablesCache` — the stamped storage the iteration fills at its end with the observables of the state it just produced (the physical velocity, vorticity, and scalar, the maximum velocity component, and, with tracers, the particle velocities), read through checked accessors that serve the deposited value or throw; nothing is ever recomputed on a read.
- [Forcing](api/Forcing.md): the interchangeable energy-injection strategies under `AbstractForcing`, chosen independently for the velocity and the scalar.
- [Generator](api/Generator.md): the flow's equations of motion — the rotational-form Navier–Stokes right-hand side (dealiased, Leray-projected nonlinear term), the passive-scalar advection, the exact viscous integrating factor, and the reified `ResolvedGenerator` — the `FlowConditions` (``\nu``, the Schmidt number, the forcings) bundled with the assembly scratch behind the protocol a scheme consumes. The physical fields its assembly consumes — the velocity, vorticity, and scalar in physical representation — are recovered by the source-preserving `to_physical!` scratch arity of `GBGPeriodicFields` and by [`vorticity_physical!`](api/Observables.md).
- [Propagator](api/Propagator.md): the integrating-factor time-stepping schemes under `AbstractIntegrator` — `IFRK2` (Heun) and the low-storage `IFLowStorageRK3`.
- [Particles](api/Particles.md): Lagrangian tracer tracking — `Tracers`, second-order `advect!` (Heun-in-time), and `sample!` for the Lagrangian statistics.
- [SimulationRun](api/SimulationRun.md): the ergonomic run layer — `Simulation` and the functions that advance it (`advance!`, `run!`, `warmup!`), report its measured flow state (`flow_parameters`), and compare its measured energy spectrum to a `Phenomenology` model (`validate_spectrum`).
- [Phenomenology](phenomenology.md): the reference turbulence laws — model spectra, structure-function laws, and scaling constants — to validate a run against. A separate, opt-in group: not re-exported by the umbrella, and designed to be extractable into a standalone package.

# Cache — the observables cache: the stamped storage where a component of the iteration
# puts a value another component (or an observation) will read. The design is the no-recompute rule
# (docs/src/observables_cache.md): the schedule is fixed at construction and identical on every
# iteration; every slot is deposited at the end of the iteration from the state it has just
# produced; a read either finds the stamped value or throws — there is no path, internal or on
# request, by which reading causes a computation. A slot IS the register its producer computes in:
# mid-iteration the step takes the array as stage scratch (clearing the stamp), and the closing deposit
# re-fills it, so caching adds nothing to what an iteration computes, moves, or stores.

"""
    Cache

The observables cache — the stamped storage where the iteration leaves the observables it produces,
for the run's own components and any observation to read. An **iteration** is one advance of the
run — one `step!` and the `deposit!` that closes it; between iterations the run stands at one
``t_n``, after one advance and before the next — an instant of the run's clock, not a face of the
periodic box; a slot's **stamp** records which of these instants its value belongs to, so a read
can be refused rather than served stale. The cache's operations:
- [`ObservablesCache`](@ref) — the cache itself: the slots, their stamps, and the epoch counters.
- [`resolved_fields_modified!`](@ref), [`particles_modified!`](@ref) — announce a state or tracer
  mutation made outside the iteration, so the affected stamps go stale and reads throw until the
  next deposit.
- [`velocity_physical`](@ref), [`vorticity_physical`](@ref), [`scalar_physical`](@ref) — the checked
  reads of the cached fields (the velocity, vorticity, and scalar in physical representation).
- `max_speed` — the cached maximum velocity component, the number the timestep choice reads (one
  generic with the field reduction in `Observables`, whose docstring is the home).
- [`particle_velocities`](@ref) — the checked read of the particle velocities
  ``\\boldsymbol{u}(t_n, \\boldsymbol{X}(t_n))``, the velocity sampled along each trajectory.

The solver's side of the contract is [`deposit!`](@ref GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Propagator.deposit!)
— homed in `Propagator`, with the iteration it closes — which fills and stamps every slot at the
iteration's end; internal per-slot deposit and take verbs move the individual stamps. A caller
reads through the accessors and never deposits.
"""
module Cache

using GBGPeriodicFields: point_values
using ..SimulationDomain: FixedDomain, new_vector_field_physical, new_scalar_field_physical
import ..Observables: max_speed           # one generic: the field reduction there, the cached read here

export ObservablesCache, velocity_physical, vorticity_physical, scalar_physical,
       particle_velocities, resolved_fields_modified!, particles_modified!

```math
\partial_t \varphi = -\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol{u} \varphi) + \varkappa\nabla^{2}\varphi + f_\varphi,
```

with ``\varkappa = \nu/\mathrm{Sc}`` the scalar diffusivity, ``\mathrm{Sc}`` the Schmidt number, and ``f_\varphi`` a forcing on the scalar. In both
equations the diffusive (stiff) term is integrated exactly through an integrating factor, the
nonlinear term explicitly by a Runge–Kutta stage combination, and the quadratic product is
dealiased by the default ``2/3`` rule (other strategies are available).

!!! note "Work in progress"
    The package is being built in vertical slices. The coupled velocity + passive-scalar solver is in
    place, advanced by the integrating-factor schemes ([`IFLowStorageRK3`](@ref) — the default — and
    [`IFRK2`](@ref)), with the forcing menu `NoForcing`, `BandRescaling`, `Linear`, and
    `ConstantPower`, and Lagrangian particle
    tracking ([`Tracers`](@ref)). The reference turbulence laws ([`Phenomenology`](phenomenology.md))
    provide usable model spectra, structure-function laws, and scaling constants; the Eulerian and
    Lagrangian statistics are being added.

## Components

- [`SimulationDomain`](api/SimulationDomain.md): the discretized periodic box a run lives on — the
  `FixedDomain`, its choice of `DealiasingStrategy`, and the scratch-field allocators.
- [`State`](api/State.md): the evolving state (`ResolvedFields`, the single source of truth) a step
  advances, and the random initial conditions — a divergence-free velocity and a mean-zero scalar.
- [`Observables`](api/Observables.md): the measured functions of state — the energy and dissipation
  diagnostics of the flow and the scalar (`kinetic_energy`, `dissipation`, …), the spectrum-derived
  scales (`energy_spectrum`, `integral_scale`), the advective-CFL `max_speed`, and the field-valued
  vorticity recovery.
- [`Cache`](api/Cache.md): the `ObservablesCache` — the stamped storage each **iteration** (one
  advance of the run: a step plus the deposit that closes it) fills at its
  end with the observables of the state it just produced; the checked accessors
  (`velocity_physical`, `max_speed`, …) serve the deposited value or throw, and nothing is
  recomputed on a read.
- [`Forcing`](api/Forcing.md): the interchangeable energy-injection strategies under `AbstractForcing`,
  chosen independently for the velocity and the scalar.
- [`Generator`](api/Generator.md): the flow's equations of motion — the rotational-form Navier–Stokes
  right-hand side, the passive-scalar advection, and the exact viscous integrating factor — reified as
  the `ResolvedGenerator` a scheme consumes through the generator protocol.
- [`Propagator`](api/Propagator.md): the interchangeable integrating-factor schemes under
  `AbstractIntegrator`.
- [`Particles`](api/Particles.md): Lagrangian tracer tracking — second-order advection and field
  sampling along trajectories, with a selectable interpolation scheme.
- [`SimulationRun`](api/SimulationRun.md): the ergonomic run layer — assembles a `Simulation` and
  runs it with `advance!` / `run!` / `warmup!` (the CFL-adaptive loop with automatic warm-up to
  stationarity); it reports the measured flow state (`flow_parameters`) and compares the measured
  energy spectrum to a `Phenomenology` model (`validate_spectrum`).
- [`Phenomenology`](phenomenology.md): the reference turbulence laws — model spectra, structure-function
  laws, and scaling constants — to validate a run against. A separate, opt-in group (not re-exported by
  the umbrella), designed to be extractable into a standalone package.
