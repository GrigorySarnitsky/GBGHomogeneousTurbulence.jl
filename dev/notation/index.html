Notation

The symbols used throughout the docstrings and the design documents, with the code object each one denotes. All mathematics is rendered LaTeX; a code identifier always appears in backticks and never inside a math span. An operation family is listed once, by its allocating name; the in-place sibling appends ! (curl / curl!). This page is the single reference — a docstring introduces a symbol on first use and need not repeat the convention.

The physical conventions are standard turbulence usage — see Pope (2000), Turbulent Flows; the field and spectral conventions follow GBGPeriodicFields, a direct dependency.

Conventions

ConceptSymbolConvention
Imaginary unit$\mathrm{i}$Upright, always — never bare italic $i$, never $\imath$. Writing it upright frees $i$ for use as an index.
Indices$i, j, a, \dots$Indices are italic and taken in order. A repeated index within a term is summed over the three components $1,2,3$ (Einstein convention).
Normalized inner product$\langle f, g \rangle$$\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega\rvert}\int_\Omega f\cdot g\,\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}$, the average over the periodic box $\Omega$ of the pointwise product. Parseval holds for it in this normalization, which is why the quadratic reductions ($k$, $\varepsilon$, the variances) take a field in either representation and return the same number.
Spatial average$\langle \cdot \rangle$The box average $\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega\rvert}\int_\Omega(\cdot)\,\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}$ over the periodic domain $\Omega$ — the single-realization estimate of the homogeneous ensemble average (exact only in the ergodic, infinite-box limit; at a finite box it retains the large-scale statistical fluctuation).
Box units$\tilde{q}$A quantity $q$ divided by its unit built from the box side $L$ and the dissipation rate $\varepsilon$: length $L$, time $(L^{2}/\varepsilon)^{1/3}$, velocity $(\varepsilon L)^{1/3}$. So $\tilde{k} = k/(\varepsilon L)^{2/3}$ and $\tilde{\nu} = \nu/(\varepsilon^{1/3}L^{4/3})$. The tilde means this and nothing else; an average is never a tilde (see the box mean below).
Box mean$\langle \cdot \rangle_l$The average over a cube of side $l$ centred at each point, so $\langle\boldsymbol{u}\rangle_l$ is a field; the unindexed $\langle\cdot\rangle$ is the average over the whole box. Averages and filters are always brackets with an index, never an overbar or a tilde.
Spectral representation$\hat f$The field $f$ in spectral (Fourier) representation, to_spectral. A field is one physical object; physical and spectral are two representations of it.
Wavenumber clash$\boldsymbol{\kappa}$ / $\kappa$ / $k$Kept distinct: bold $\boldsymbol{\kappa}$ is the wavenumber vector, $\kappa = \lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert$ its magnitude (the spectrum coordinate), and italic scalar $k$ is the turbulent kinetic energy.

Vocabulary

The page above fixes one symbol per physical concept. This section does the same for the package's operational words — the ones that name how a run is organized rather than what it computes. Each is used throughout the docstrings, and each means exactly this and nothing looser.

TermMeaning
iterationone advance of the run, in this order: choose the timestep, step! the fields, deposit! to close them, then advance any tracers and record their sample — the tracers come last because their second velocity sample is interpolated from the field the deposit just made current. An iteration is wider than a step, which is only the first half, and than a stage, which is one Runge–Kutta substep inside it.
depositto write an observable into the cache and mark the moment it belongs to, in one act — the fields and the tracer positions each advance separately, and each advance is closed by its own deposit! form, the field form after the step and the particle form after the tracer push, inside the same iteration. Nothing is ever written to the cache on a read.
slotone storage place in the ObservablesCache — an array or a number, together with the epoch its value belongs to.
epocha counter of changes to the state, one per dynamical factor (the fields, the tracer positions) — and, for a slot, the value that counter held when the slot was filled. A slot is readable exactly while the epoch it records still equals the counter it depends on. Not an iteration number: a change to the state made outside a step and announced through fields_modified! advances an epoch without advancing the clock.
registerstorage a component computes one value into and overwrites at the next use, holding nothing a reader should trust in between. A cache slot doubles as its producer's register mid-iteration, which is why a cached field is meaningful only between iterations.
scratchworking storage a component owns for its intermediate results — a register, or a named bundle of them (the generator's assembly scratch, a scheme's IntegratorBuffers).
pushone advance of the tracer positions — the particle half of an iteration, performed by advect!.
front doorthe former name for what current docs call the domain-building Simulation constructor: the form that builds every component from a few keywords, as against handing it a pre-built domain. In a wider sense it also names SimulationRun, the layer a user enters the package through.
holderin the test suite's audit of costs against their lower bounds: the named owner of a gap between an achieved cost and its floor — every residual distance must carry one, and closing the gap retires it in the same commit.
ledgera frozen table of measured costs (memory, transforms, passes, synchronizations) the test suite holds the code to; a deliberate change re-freezes it consciously, citing its decision.
floorthe physical lower bound a ledger cell is held against — what the cost could be, as opposed to what it is.
charta physical-representation view of the state as held in spectral representation — the velocity, vorticity or scalar recovered from it. The cache's field slots are the run's charts; the chart map is the recovery itself.
resolvedin ResolvedFields and ResolvedGenerator, the Mori–Zwanzig sense: the fully resolved dynamics, as opposed to a filtered one — the eventual LES sibling would be a FilteredGenerator. The numerical sense — a scale the grid resolves, as in the resolution criterion $\kappa_{\max}\eta$ — is a different reading, and the docs write it out in full wherever it is meant.
