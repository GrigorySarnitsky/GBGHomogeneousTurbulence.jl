## The discretized box

The reusable, read-only frame every buffer is allocated from — the `FixedDomain`, a fixed, unsheared
periodic cube (a sheared frame is deferred).

```@docs
FixedDomain
```

## Dealiasing strategies

The aliasing-control strategy passed to `FixedDomain` — the supertype, then the sharp ``2/3``-rule
default, the smooth Hou–Li filter, and the exact half-cell phase-shift with spherical truncation.

```@docs

TwoThirds
HouLi
PhaseShift
```

The dealiasing cutoff it sets — the highest wavenumber the domain evolves, and the resolution
criterion ``\kappa_{\max}\eta`` a run is built to meet.

# ── Dealiasing strategies ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# How the aliasing of the quadratic nonlinearity is controlled. Each strategy builds the `domain.dealias`
# kernel (applied by `dealias!`/`convolve!`) and declares the Nyquist fraction it keeps energetic,
# which sets the advective-CFL ceiling (see `recommended_cfl`).

"""
    DealiasingStrategy

How the aliasing of the quadratic nonlinearity is controlled. A [`FixedDomain`](@ref) is built
with one; it fixes the `domain.dealias` kernel and, through `recommended_cfl`, the advective-CFL
ceiling. Concrete strategies: [`TwoThirds`](@ref) (the sharp ``2/3`` rule, the default),
[`HouLi`](@ref) (the smooth Hou–Li filter), and [`PhaseShift`](@ref) (the half-cell shift-average,
truncated spherically at ``\\tfrac{2\\sqrt{2}}{3}\\kappa_{\\mathrm{Ny}}``).
"""
abstract type DealiasingStrategy end

"""
    TwoThirds()

The sharp isotropic ``2/3``-rule truncation: keep the modes
``\\kappa \\le \\kappa_{\\max} = \\tfrac{2}{3}\\kappa_{\\mathrm{Ny}}`` and zero the rest, where
``\\kappa_{\\mathrm{Ny}} = \\tfrac{N}{2}\\cdot\\tfrac{2\\pi}{L}`` is the Nyquist wavenumber and
``\\kappa_{\\max}`` the dealiased maximum — two-thirds of the way out to Nyquist. Exact dealiasing
of the quadratic nonlinearity, and the lean, GPU-friendly default. The rule is due to Orszag (1971).
"""
struct TwoThirds <: DealiasingStrategy end
