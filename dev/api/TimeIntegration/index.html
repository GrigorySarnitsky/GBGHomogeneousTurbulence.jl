TimeIntegration

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration — Module
TimeIntegration

The pluggable time-marching schemes, as concrete AbstractIntegrator subtypes dispatched by step!. All are of the integrating-factor family: the stiff diffusive part is advanced exactly by the integrating factor $\mathcal{E} = \exp(-\nu\lvert\mathbf{k}\rvert^{2}\Delta t)$ and the nonlinear term explicitly by a Runge–Kutta stage combination.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.AbstractIntegrator — Type
AbstractIntegrator

Supertype of the time-marching schemes. A scheme implements step!(integrator, uh, ops, ws, forcing, viscosity, dt), advancing the velocity field uh (in spectral representation) by one timestep in place.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.IFRK2 — Type
IFRK2()

Integrating-factor second-order Runge–Kutta (Heun). With the integrating factor $\mathcal{E} = \exp(-\nu\lvert\mathbf{k}\rvert^{2}\Delta t)$ and $\widehat{\mathbf{N}}$ the de-aliased Leray-projected nonlinearity (see velocity_nonlinear!), one step of the velocity field $\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ is

\[\begin{aligned} \widehat{\mathbf{N}}_1 &= \widehat{\mathbf{N}}(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_n), & \hat{\mathbf{u}}^{*} &= \mathcal{E}\,(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_n + \Delta t\,\widehat{\mathbf{N}}_1), \\ \widehat{\mathbf{N}}_2 &= \widehat{\mathbf{N}}(\hat{\mathbf{u}}^{*}), & \hat{\mathbf{u}}_{n+1} &= \mathcal{E}\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_n + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}\big(\mathcal{E}\,\widehat{\mathbf{N}}_1 + \widehat{\mathbf{N}}_2\big), \end{aligned}\]

after which the forcing is applied. Two nonlinear evaluations per step, minimal storage.

Note

Every two-stage explicit Runge–Kutta has stability function $R(z) = 1 + z + z^{2}/2$, for which $\lvert R(iy)\rvert^{2} = 1 + y^{4}/4 > 1$: the scheme is unstable on the imaginary axis, so it is stable only where the viscous damping of the integrating factor dominates. It is adequate for well-resolved, modest-Reynolds-number runs but not the workhorse — prefer the low-storage RK3 for high Reynolds number or marginal resolution.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.step! — Function
step!(integrator, uh, ops, ws, forcing, viscosity, dt)

Advance the spectral velocity field uh by one timestep dt in place, using the scheme integrator, the operators ops, the workspace ws, the forcing strategy, and the kinematic viscosity $\nu$. Dispatches on the concrete integrator type (see IFRK2). The caller must have filled ws.velocity and ws.vorticity_start with the start-of-step physical velocity and vorticity (via velocity_vorticity!).
