Generator

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator — Module
Generator

The flow's equations of motion — the incompressible Navier–Stokes right-hand side in rotational form (the de-aliased, Leray-projected nonlinear term), the passive-scalar advection, and the exact viscous integrating factor. In the Mori–Zwanzig reading (design doc §8) this is the generator of the full dynamics — it advances the flow state; Observables are the functions of that state, and Propagator the numerical propagator.

The module has six operations — velocity_to_physical! and vorticity_to_physical! recover the physical velocity and vorticity, and scalar_to_physical! the physical scalar, from their spectral representations; velocity_nonlinear! and scalar_nonlinear! assemble the de-aliased momentum and scalar nonlinear terms; and integrating_factor! builds the exact viscous/diffusive spectral multiplier.

Every field-transforming or nonlinear-assembly method takes the operator bundle domain (a FixedDomain: grid, transform plans, wavenumber multipliers, de-alias kernel) and the preallocated scratch buffers (a FieldBuffers) as its final two arguments; integrating_factor! takes domain alone (no buffers).

Flow conditions

The physical conditions the generator is built from — viscosity, Schmidt number, and the velocity and scalar forcings — that step! consumes.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.FlowConditions — Type
FlowConditions(; viscosity, schmidt = 1.0, velocity_forcing = NoForcing(),
               scalar_forcing = NoForcing())

The physical conditions the generator is built from — the kinematic viscosity $\nu$ (viscosity; the Reynolds number is $\mathrm{Re} = 1/\nu$), the Schmidt number $\mathrm{Sc}$ (schmidt; the scalar diffusivity is $\varkappa = \nu/\mathrm{Sc}$), and the velocity and scalar forcing strategies (velocity_forcing, scalar_forcing, both AbstractForcing). Consumed by the time step (step!).

Physical-field evaluation

Recover the physical velocity, vorticity, and scalar φ that the nonlinear terms consume from the spectral state (the spectral sources are preserved).

Compute the physical vorticity $\boldsymbol{\omega} = \nabla\times\boldsymbol{u}$ from the velocity field û (given in spectral representation $\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$) into the buffer ω — the spectral vorticity $\mathrm{i}\boldsymbol{\kappa}\times\hat{\boldsymbol{u}}$ is formed in scratch and transformed, leaving û untouched.

Nonlinear-term assembly

The de-aliased, Leray-projected rotational-form momentum term, and the conservative scalar-advection term.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.velocity_nonlinear! — Function
velocity_nonlinear!(nonlinear, u, ω, domain, buffers)

Assemble the de-aliased, Leray-projected nonlinear term of the momentum equation into nonlinear, from the physical u $\boldsymbol{u}$ and ω $\boldsymbol{\omega}$.

In physical space the rotational form writes the convective term as the Lamb vector: with the identity $(\boldsymbol{u}\cdot\nabla)\boldsymbol{u} = \nabla\big(\tfrac12\lvert\boldsymbol{u}\rvert^{2}\big) - \boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega}$, the divergence-free momentum equation becomes $\partial_t\boldsymbol{u} = \mathbb{P}\,(\boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega}) + \nu\nabla^{2}\boldsymbol{u} + \boldsymbol{f}$, where the Leray–Helmholtz projection $\mathbb{P}$ onto divergence-free fields absorbs the pressure and the $\nabla(\tfrac12\lvert\boldsymbol{u}\rvert^{2})$ gradient. The term computed here is the spectral image of $\mathbb{P}\,(\boldsymbol{u}\times\boldsymbol{\omega})$, with the de-alias kernel domain.dealias (the sharp $2/3$ rule by default) controlling the aliasing of the quadratic product. Allocation-free (a destination distinct from its sources needs no internal scratch).

source
GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.scalar_nonlinear! — Function
scalar_nonlinear!(nonlinear, u, φ, domain, buffers)

Assemble the de-aliased advection term of the passive-scalar equation into nonlinear, from the physical u $\boldsymbol{u}$ and physical φ $\varphi$.

In conservative form the advection is $-\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol{u}\varphi)$ (equal to $-\boldsymbol{u}\cdot\nabla\varphi$ since $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol{u} = 0$); the conservative form is used because it conserves the scalar mean exactly — the divergence has no $\boldsymbol{\kappa}=\boldsymbol 0$ component, and the de-alias kernel domain.dealias (the sharp $2/3$ rule by default) leaves that mode untouched. It is computed pseudospectrally as $-\,\mathrm{dealias}\big(\mathrm{i}\boldsymbol{\kappa}\cdot\widehat{\boldsymbol{u}\varphi}\big)$, the kernel controlling the aliasing of the quadratic product. The scalar flux $\boldsymbol{u}\varphi$ reuses the physical and spectral velocity scratch.

Integrating factor

The exact per-mode diffusive propagator — ν for the velocity, ν/Sc for the scalar — optionally folding in a linear forcing rate.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.Generator.integrating_factor! — Function
integrating_factor!(factor, domain, diffusivity, dt, rate = nothing)

Write the exact diffusive integrating factor into the scalar spectral buffer factor. Over a step $\Delta t$ (dt) the diffusion equation $\partial_t f = D\nabla^{2} f$ is solved exactly in spectral representation by the per-mode multiplier

\[\mathcal{E}(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) = \exp\!\big(-D\,\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2}\,\Delta t\big),\]

so multiplying a field by factor advances its viscous/diffusive part exactly (no stability restriction). The operator bundle domain (a FixedDomain) supplies the squared-wavenumber field $\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2}$ (domain.squared_wavenumber), the symbol of $-\nabla^{2}$. The diffusivity $D$ (diffusivity) is the kinematic viscosity $\nu$ for the velocity and $\nu/\mathrm{Sc}$ for the scalar $\varphi$ ($\mathrm{Sc}$ the Schmidt number).

When a linear forcing rate $A(\boldsymbol{\kappa})$ is supplied (rate, a real array; from the set_forcing_rate! of a Linear/ConstantPower forcing), it is folded into the linear operator, $\mathcal{E}(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) = \exp\!\big((A(\boldsymbol{\kappa}) - D\lvert\boldsymbol{\kappa}\rvert^{2})\,\Delta t\big)$ — exact for the linear forcing, so the integrator keeps its order.
