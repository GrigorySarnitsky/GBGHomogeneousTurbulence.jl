TimeIntegration

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration — Module
TimeIntegration

The pluggable time-marching schemes, as concrete AbstractIntegrator subtypes dispatched by step!, which advances the coupled velocity + passive-scalar state. All are of the integrating-factor family: the stiff diffusive part is advanced exactly and the nonlinear term explicitly by a Runge–Kutta stage combination. The default is IFLowStorageRK3; IFRK2 (Heun) is a cheaper two-stage alternative, imaginary-axis unstable; default_integrator returns the default; recommended_cfl gives a scheme's advective-CFL ceiling; Physics bundles the problem parameters and forcings.

Integrator schemes

The integrating-factor scheme supertype and its concretes — the default third-order low-storage RK3, then the cheaper, marginally-stable second-order Heun.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.AbstractIntegrator — Type
AbstractIntegrator

Supertype of the time-marching schemes. A scheme implements step!(integrator, û, φ̂, domain, buffers, physics, dt), advancing the velocity û and the scalar φ̂ (both in spectral representation) by one timestep in place, and may specialize recommended_cfl.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.IFLowStorageRK3 — Type
IFLowStorageRK3()

Integrating-factor third-order low-storage Runge–Kutta (Williamson 2N). With the diffusive integrating factor $\mathcal{E}_D(\tau)$ built by integrating_factor! over a substep interval $\tau$ ($D = \nu$ for the velocity, $\varkappa = \nu/\mathrm{Sc}$ for the scalar) and $\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}$ the de-aliased nonlinearity of each field, the step carries a state $\hat q$ and a single accumulator $\boldsymbol{h}$ through three stages $i = 1,2,3$,

\[\begin{aligned} \boldsymbol{h} &\leftarrow A_i\,\boldsymbol{h} + \Delta t\,\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}(\hat q), \\ \hat q &\leftarrow \hat q + B_i\,\boldsymbol{h}, \\ \hat q &\leftarrow \mathcal{E}_D(d_i\Delta t)\,\hat q, \\ \boldsymbol{h} &\leftarrow \mathcal{E}_D(d_i\Delta t)\,\boldsymbol{h}, \end{aligned}\]

with $A = (0, -5/9, -153/128)$, $B = (1/3, 15/16, 8/15)$, and node increments $d = (1/3, 5/12, 1/4)$ (abscissae $c = (0, 1/3, 3/4)$). The velocity and scalar are advanced in lockstep (the same $A,B,d$), each with its own diffusivity; setting $D = 0$ recovers the bare Williamson scheme, and dropping the nonlinearity recovers exact diffusion $\mathcal{E}_D(\Delta t)$. Three nonlinear evaluations per step, two field registers per field — the default workhorse: third-order and stable on the imaginary axis (limit $\sqrt{3}$), unlike IFRK2.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.IFRK2 — Type
IFRK2()

Integrating-factor second-order Runge–Kutta (Heun). With the velocity and scalar integrating factors $\mathcal{E}_\nu$ and $\mathcal{E}_\varkappa$ (the diffusive kernel of integrating_factor! at $D = \nu$ and $D = \varkappa = \nu/\mathrm{Sc}$ over the step $\Delta t$) and $\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}$ each field's de-aliased nonlinearity, one step of a field $\hat q$ with integrating factor $\mathcal{E}$ is

\[\begin{aligned} \hat q^{*} &= \mathcal{E}\,(\hat q_n + \Delta t\,\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}_1), \\ \hat q_{n+1} &= \mathcal{E}\,\hat q_n + \tfrac{\Delta t}{2}\big(\mathcal{E}\,\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}_1 + \widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}_2\big), \end{aligned}\]

with $\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}_1$ at $\hat q_n$ and $\widehat{\boldsymbol{N}}_2$ at the predictor $\hat q^{*}$; velocity and scalar use $\mathcal{E}_\nu$ and $\mathcal{E}_\varkappa$ respectively, then the forcings are applied. Two nonlinear evaluations per step.

Note

Every second-order two-stage explicit Runge–Kutta has stability function $R(z) = 1 + z + z^{2}/2$, for which $\lvert R(\mathrm{i}y)\rvert^{2} = 1 + y^{4}/4 > 1$: the scheme is unstable on the imaginary axis, so it is stable only where the viscous damping of the integrating factor dominates. It is adequate for well-resolved, modest-Reynolds-number runs but is not the default — prefer IFLowStorageRK3 for high Reynolds number or marginal resolution.

Time stepping

One in-place step of the coupled velocity and passive scalar together through the shared RK stages.

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.step! — Function
step!(integrator, û, φ̂, domain, buffers, physics, dt)

Advance the spectral velocity û and passive scalar φ̂ by one timestep dt in place, using the scheme integrator, the domain domain, the buffers buffers, and the problem physics (a Physics: viscosity, Schmidt number, and the two forcings). Dispatches on the concrete integrator type (see IFRK2, IFLowStorageRK3). The caller must have filled buffers.velocity and buffers.vorticity_start with the start-of-step physical velocity and vorticity (via velocity_vorticity!).

GBGIsotropicTurbulence.TimeIntegration.recommended_cfl — Function
recommended_cfl(integrator)
recommended_cfl(integrator, domain)

A conservative advective-CFL ceiling $C$ for the scheme, for a timestep $\Delta t = C\,\Delta x / \max_i\lvert u_i\rvert$.

The advective eigenvalues of the de-aliased system are imaginary, of magnitude up to $\kappa_{\max}\max\lvert\boldsymbol{u}\rvert$; stability needs this times $C$ to stay within the scheme's imaginary-axis stability limit $y_{\mathrm{s}}$. The one-argument form returns the value calibrated for the $2/3$ rule ($\kappa_{\max}\Delta x = 2\pi/3 \approx 2.09$): for IFLowStorageRK3 $y_{\mathrm{s}} = \sqrt{3}$ gives $C \lesssim 0.83$, taken conservatively as $0.5$; IFRK2 is imaginary-axis unstable ($y_{\mathrm{s}} = 0$, relying on viscous damping), so a small $0.25$.

The two-argument form scales that ceiling by the domain's de-aliasing: a strategy that keeps modes to a higher $\kappa_{\max}$ (e.g. HouLi, out to the Nyquist wavenumber) takes a proportionally smaller step, $C \propto 1/\kappa_{\max}$. This is the value Simulation uses. Override either for a specific run.
